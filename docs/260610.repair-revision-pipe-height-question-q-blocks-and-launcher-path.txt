OBJECTIVE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Visual inspection of the prior generated chunks revealed three
remaining issues:

   (1) Pipes were either too short (a 2-row stub with only 1 body
       row) or implausibly tall (an 8 to 12 row tower). Real SMB
       pipes are typically 3 to 5 rows.

   (2) QuestionEmpty (Q in VGLC) blocks were embedded in solid
       ground or floating in mid-air without sufficient clearance.
       The previous repair only applied placement rules to
       QuestionFull (?) blocks even though Q is more common in the
       training data and reads visually as a hit-? block.

   (3) Pipes had only 1 row of head clearance above the cap. Mario
       big is 2 tiles tall; entering from above requires 2 rows of
       head clearance.

   (4) Bullet bill launcher had only 3 cells of horizontal clearance
       which left a wall just past the clearance zone that
       immediately blocked the bullet trajectory.

This revision implements:

   New rules: MinPipeRows = 3, MaxPipeRows = 5, removal of the
   must-have-at-least-one override.

   QuestionBlockRepair applies the same placement rules to both
   QuestionFull and QuestionEmpty.

   PipeTopClearance now requires 2 cells of head clearance above
   the cap.

   BulletBillLauncherClearance extends to a 14-tile single-direction
   clearance path.

PART 1: PIPE HEIGHT BOUNDS [3, 5]
================================================================================

1.1 RULES

   Minimum pipe height: 3 tiles (top + 2 body rows). The canonical
   short SMB pipe is 3 tiles tall; shorter pipes look stunted and
   break the SMB silhouette.

   Maximum pipe height: 5 tiles. This matches Mario big's jump
   reach from the ground (about 4 tiles upward). Pipes taller than
   5 tiles cannot be landed on from the ground without external
   scenery, which the chunk does not guarantee.

   The must-have-at-least-one override is removed: previously,
   PipeSupportRepair extended the first floating pipe regardless
   of height to guarantee chunks had at least one pipe. With
   PipeInjection now reliably injecting short pipes near the
   ground when needed, the override is no longer necessary. Tall
   pipes are now strictly removed.

1.2 ENFORCEMENT POINTS

   PipeRepair early-reject: PipeTopLeft at y >= chunk.Height - 3
   cannot satisfy MinPipeRows = 3 without writing body into the
   ground row. The PipeTopLeft is replaced.

   PipeRepair completion: FillCompletion now writes 5 tiles
   (right cap + 2 body rows on both columns) instead of 3 tiles.
   The completion produces a 3-row pipe directly.

   PipeSupportRepair RemoveTooShortPipes: scans complete pipe
   anchors at the top of each pass and removes any whose total
   column height is below MinPipeRows.

   PipeSupportRepair main loop: strict MaxPipeRows enforcement
   without override. WouldFitMaxPipeHeight rejects pipes whose
   extension to ground would exceed 5 rows.

   PipeInjection: InjectedPipeHeight = 3, matching MinPipeRows.

PART 2: QUESTIONEMPTY VALIDITY
================================================================================

2.1 RULE

QuestionBlockRepair now applies the same placement validity rules
to both QuestionFull and QuestionEmpty:

   Not on the bottom row.
   3 cells of clearance directly below (HasEmptyDirectlyBelow).
   Not buried in solid (at least 2 of 4 cardinal neighbors Empty).
   Has Solid or Breakable within 8 rows below (HasGroundBelow).
   Not adjacent to pipe or bullet bill structures.

2.2 RATIONALE

QuestionEmpty (Q in VGLC encoding) appears 1788 times in the
training corpus, more than twice as often as QuestionFull (768
times). Level designers pre-place Q as decorative hit-blocks at
jump height; visually they read the same as ? blocks. A Q embedded
in solid ground looks just as wrong as a ? embedded in solid
ground, so the placement rules apply equally.


================================================================================
PART 3: PIPE TOP 2-CELL HEAD CLEARANCE
================================================================================

3.1 RULE

For each complete PipeTopLeft + PipeTopRight anchor, the cells at
both (x, y-1) AND (x, y-2) above the cap must be Empty. Solid,
Breakable, Coin, Question*, and Enemy tiles in either of those
two rows are replaced with Empty.

3.2 RATIONALE

Mario big is 2 tiles tall. To enter a pipe from above, both his
feet position (immediately above the pipe cap) and his head
position (one cell higher) must be unobstructed. A single cell of
clearance lets Mario small enter but not Mario big.

3.3 UNDERGROUND CEILING INTERACTION

The Underground biome guard now widens to y <= 2: a pipe at y == 1
or y == 2 in an Underground chunk cannot have 2 cells of clearance
without overwriting the cave ceiling. Those pipes are skipped by
PipeTopClearance and flagged as structurally implausible edge cases.


================================================================================
PART 4: LAUNCHER 14-TILE PATH
================================================================================

4.1 RULE

For each BulletBillLauncher, the pass clears Solid and Breakable
tiles up to LongClearanceTiles = 14 cells (or until the chunk
edge) on the longer side of the launcher (the side with more
distance to the chunk edge). The shorter side is not modified.

4.2 RATIONALE

Real SMB bullet bill cannons sit at the end of long open stretches
so bullets have travel distance before reaching the screen edge
or Mario. A 3-cell symmetric clearance left a wall just past the
clearance zone, immediately blocking the trajectory. A 14-cell
single-direction clearance gives meaningful travel distance and
matches the half-chunk visual rule the user proposed.
