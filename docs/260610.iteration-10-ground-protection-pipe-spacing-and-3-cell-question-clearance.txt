PART 1: DESIGN - THREE REFINEMENTS
================================================================================

1.1 GROUND-ROW PROTECTION FOR PIPES AND BULLET BILLS

The Iteration 5-9 PipeRepair completed pipe templates by writing
PipeBodyLeft/PipeBodyRight over any "benign" target (Empty or Solid).
A PipeTopLeft placed by the model at chunk.Height - 2 was completed
with its body at chunk.Height - 1, which is the ground row. The body
displaced the ground Solid that BottomRowCompletion had placed,
visually breaking ground continuity.

Iteration 10 adds an early-reject rule to PipeRepair: if PipeTopLeft
is at y >= chunk.Height - 2, the completion is rejected and the
PipeTopLeft is replaced via SelectReplacementTile. Pipes must attach
ABOVE the ground row, not in it.

PipeSupportRepair's extension loop is similarly tightened. Body must
never be written at chunk.Height - 1; if extension reaches the
would-be ground row without finding a ground tile already there,
the method returns false and the caller removes the entire pipe
column.

BulletBillRepair and BulletBillSupportRepair get the mirror
treatment for 1-tile-wide bullet bill columns.

1.2 MINIMUM SPACING BETWEEN INJECTED PIPES

The Iteration 9 PipeInjection scanned columns left-to-right and
placed pipes at the first valid spot. With a target count of 3 and
no spacing rule, three pipes ended up at columns 0-1, 2-3, 4-5
(touching) in many chunks. Real SMB pipes are always separated by
several tiles of walkable ground.

Iteration 10 adds a MinSpacingColumns = 2 constant and a
HasNearbyPipeStructure check. Before injecting at column x, the pass
scans columns x-2 through x-1 (left side) and x+2 through x+3 (right
side) for any pipe tile in any row from pipeTopY down to ground. If
any pipe tile is present, the candidate column is skipped. The scan
continues to the next candidate column without abandoning the
injection.

The effect: injected pipes are at least 2 Empty columns apart from
each other (and from model-placed pipes). In a 28-column chunk with
3 target pipes, the typical injected positions are 0-1, 4-5, 8-9.

1.3 3-CELL CLEARANCE BELOW QUESTIONFULL

The Iteration 9 HasEmptyDirectlyBelow rule only required (x, y+1) to
be Empty. A QuestionFull at y with Solid at y+2 or y+3 still passed,
even though Solid in those rows blocks Mario big's jump-arc head
clearance.

Iteration 10 tightens the rule: all three cells (x, y+1), (x, y+2),
(x, y+3) must be Empty. This matches Mario big's hitbox sweep during
the jump-and-hit motion: his head passes through y+2 and y+1 on the
way up, so any Solid or Breakable tile in y+1..y+3 blocks the jump.

HasGroundBelow now searches from y+4 to y+8 because y+1..y+3 are
constrained to be Empty; a ground tile in that range would violate
the clearance and is not a legal way to satisfy the support
condition.

PART 2: IMPLEMENTATION
================================================================================

2.1 UPDATED: PipeRepair.cs

Added the chunk-Height-2 early-reject check at the top of the
PipeTopLeft anchoring loop. The PipeTopLeft at the rejection
position is replaced via SelectReplacementTile, mirroring the
removal path used elsewhere in the class.

2.2 UPDATED: PipeSupportRepair.cs

TryExtendPipeBodyToGround introduces maxBodyY = chunk.Height - 2.
The loop returns false (removal) if the extension would write a
body row into the ground row. The previous behavior returned true
on reaching chunk.Height without finding ground, treating the
implicit ground below the chunk as support; the new behavior treats
the bottom row as the required ground location.

2.3 UPDATED: BulletBillRepair.cs

Mirror of 2.1: early-reject when launcher is at y >= chunk.Height - 2.

2.4 UPDATED: BulletBillSupportRepair.cs

Mirror of 2.2.

2.5 UPDATED: PipeInjection.cs

Added MinSpacingColumns = 2 constant and HasNearbyPipeStructure
private method. The TryInjectOnePipe loop calls HasNearbyPipeStructure
for each candidate column and skips the column when the check
returns true.

2.6 UPDATED: QuestionBlockRepair.cs

HasEmptyDirectlyBelow now iterates dy = 1 through 3 and requires all
three cells to be Empty. HasGroundBelow starts at dy = 4 instead of
dy = 2.
