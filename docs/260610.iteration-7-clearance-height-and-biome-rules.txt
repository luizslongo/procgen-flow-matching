OBJECTIVE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

The Iteration 6 repair pipeline eliminated all floating pipes, floating
bullet bills, and floating enemies in the generated Overworld chunks.
Visual inspection of the Iteration 6 PNGs surfaced six new visual
problems that the support and structural-integrity validations of
Iterations 5 and 6 did not catch:

   (P1) Pipes with Solid or Breakable tiles directly above the cap,
        which block Mario's entry into the pipe. Observed in
        generated-png-iter6-overworld/chunk-001.png with a Breakable
        sitting on top of a green pipe at the right edge of the chunk.

   (P2) Bullet bill cannons extended into 5 and 6 tile tall columns
        because the launcher was placed many rows above the ground
        and the unbounded EXTEND policy filled every intermediate
        row with BulletBillBody. Observed in
        generated-png-iter6-overworld/chunk-004.png with three such
        columns of length 4, 5, and 5.

   (P3) The model substituting structural tiles (BulletBillBody, Coin,
        QuestionFull) for ground in bottom-row positions where the
        canonical content would be Solid. Observed in
        generated-png-iter6-overworld/chunk-006.png and chunk-010.png.

   (P4) Every Treetop chunk having a uniform continuous brick floor.
        Real SMB Treetop levels (3-3, 5-3) have explicit ground gaps
        as a gameplay feature. The BottomRowCompletion pass was
        over-applying to Treetop.

   (P5) Every Underground chunk missing the cave ceiling that defines
        the biome visually. Real SMB Underground (1-2, 4-2) renders
        the play area as a cave with a Solid roof at y=0.

   (P6) Implicit problem: PipeTopClearance interacting with the new
        ceiling-fill in Underground, where the pipe-cap clearance
        would erase the ceiling Solid and the next iteration would
        re-add it, causing oscillation.

Iteration 7 addresses (P1) through (P6) via four new repair classes
plus updates to two existing classes.

PART 1: DESIGN - SIX NEW CONSTRAINTS
================================================================================

1.1 PIPE TOP CLEARANCE (addresses P1)

For each complete PipeTopLeft + PipeTopRight anchor at (x, y), the
cells (x, y-1) and (x+1, y-1) must be Empty (or out-of-chunk). Tiles
other than Empty in these cells block Mario's entry into the pipe and
are replaced with Empty. Tiles that are themselves part of another
pipe or bullet bill structure are NOT cleared; subsequent passes
handle conflicts.

1.2 PIPE HEIGHT CAP (addresses P2 for pipes)

A pipe column cannot exceed MaxPipeRows tiles total (default 6: top
row plus body rows). Before PipeSupportRepair extends a floating
pipe to ground, it computes the would-be total height; if above the
cap, REMOVE replaces EXTEND regardless of the COMPLETE-vs-REMOVE
count policy.

1.3 BULLET BILL HEIGHT CAP (addresses P2 for bullet bills)

Mirror of 1.2 for bullet bill columns. MaxBulletBillRows defaults to 3
(launcher plus body rows), matching canonical SMB cannon heights of
2 to 3 tiles.

1.4 BOTTOM-ROW TILE FILTER (addresses P3)

Updated BottomRowCompletion replaces any tile in the bottom row that
is not Solid, Breakable, PipeBodyLeft, PipeBodyRight, or BulletBillBody
with Solid. Previously the pass only replaced Empty tiles. The new
allowed-list eliminates the model's behavior of substituting Coin,
QuestionFull, QuestionEmpty, or Enemy tiles in ground positions to
avoid DiscontinuousGround violations.

1.5 BIOME-AWARE BOTTOM-ROW COMPLETION (addresses P4)

BottomRowCompletion now checks chunk.BiomeLabel and skips the entire
pass when the biome is Treetop. Treetop chunks retain their natural
ground gaps from the model output, matching the gameplay feature of
the source SMB Treetop levels.

1.6 UNDERGROUND CEILING (addresses P5)

The new CeilingCompletion class mirrors BottomRowCompletion at the
top row, applied only when the biome is Underground. The same
allowed-list (Solid, Breakable, PipeBodyLeft, PipeBodyRight,
BulletBillBody) governs the top row. Empty and other tile types
become Solid, forming the canonical cave roof.

1.7 PIPE-CEILING ANTI-OSCILLATION (addresses P6)

PipeTopClearance now contains an Underground-specific guard: when
the pipe top is at y=1 in an Underground chunk, the clearance attempt
is skipped because the row above (y=0) is the cave ceiling enforced
by CeilingCompletion. Without this guard the two passes would
oscillate (clearance removes the Solid; ceiling re-adds it; the
fixed-point iteration never terminates).


================================================================================
PART 2: IMPLEMENTATION - TWO NEW CLASSES AND UPDATES TO FOUR EXISTING
================================================================================

2.1 NEW: PipeTopClearance.cs (93 lines)

Single-pass repair. Scans each PipeTopLeft and verifies the
adjacent PipeTopRight to confirm a complete cap. For each such cap,
calls ClearIfObstruction on (x, y-1) and (x+1, y-1). The clear method
replaces Solid, Breakable, Coin, Question*, and Enemy tiles with
Empty; pipe and bullet bill tiles are skipped to avoid breaking
other structures (orphan-tile sweep in subsequent passes catches
them if they end up disconnected).

2.2 NEW: CeilingCompletion.cs (59 lines)

Single-pass repair. Returns immediately when chunk.BiomeLabel is not
Underground. Otherwise scans the top row (y=0) and replaces every
disallowed tile with Solid. Allowed tiles: Solid, Breakable,
PipeBodyLeft, PipeBodyRight, BulletBillBody.

2.3 UPDATED: BottomRowCompletion.cs

Now biome-aware (skips Treetop) and tile-type aware (replaces every
non-allowed tile with Solid). Allowed tiles match CeilingCompletion's
list.

2.4 UPDATED: PipeSupportRepair.cs

Adds WouldFitMaxPipeHeight pre-check before invoking the
EXTEND-vs-REMOVE decision. Computes what the total pipe column height
would be after extending to the nearest ground tile below; if above
config.MaxPipeRows, the EXTEND option is rejected and the pipe is
removed via the existing REMOVE path.

2.5 UPDATED: BulletBillSupportRepair.cs

Mirror change with WouldFitMaxBulletBillHeight and config.MaxBulletBillRows.

2.6 UPDATED: ChunkStructureRepairConfig.cs

Adds MaxPipeRows = 6 and MaxBulletBillRows = 3 public fields. Default
values chosen to match canonical SMB structure heights with a small
margin for legitimately tall pipes.

PART 3: INTEGRATION - UPDATED ORCHESTRATOR PASS ORDER
================================================================================

ChunkStructureRepair.RepairAll now invokes NINE passes per iteration:

   (1) BottomRowCompletion
   (2) CeilingCompletion
   (3) PipeRepair
   (4) PipeSupportRepair
   (5) PipeTopClearance
   (6) BulletBillRepair
   (7) BulletBillSupportRepair
   (8) EnemyRepair
   (9) QuestionBlockRepair

Pass order rationale:

   Ground and ceiling (1, 2) run first so support validations have
   stable surfaces to test against.

   PipeRepair (3) anchors complete 2x2 pipes before PipeSupportRepair
   (4) verifies ground support. PipeTopClearance (5) runs LAST among
   the pipe passes so it only acts on pipes that survived both the
   structure-validity and support checks.

   BulletBill (6, 7) and Enemy (8) repairs follow the same anchor-then-
   validate pattern.

   QuestionBlockRepair (9) runs last because its adjacency rules
   depend on the final pipe and bullet bill positions.

PART 6: LIMITATIONS
================================================================================

6.1 PIPE-CEILING EDGE CASE IN UNDERGROUND

A pipe at y=1 in an Underground chunk has its cap immediately below
the cave ceiling. PipeTopClearance skips the clearance attempt to
avoid oscillation with CeilingCompletion. The pipe is structurally
implausible (the ceiling blocks entry) but it survives in the
repaired output. Frequency is expected to be very low because the
model rarely places pipe tops at y=1.

6.2 HEIGHT CAPS ARE ABSOLUTE, NOT BIOME-SPECIFIC

MaxPipeRows and MaxBulletBillRows are global config values. Real SMB
Underground levels have notably taller pipes than Overworld in some
places. A future refinement could make the height caps biome-aware.
Current values are conservative on the short side which matches the
visually-clearer Iteration 7 goal.

6.3 BIOME LABEL ASSUMED CORRECT

The biome-aware passes trust chunk.BiomeLabel, which is set by
TileMapChunker from the source VGLC level filename via
BiomeFromVglcLevelName. If the level-to-biome mapping is wrong for
any source level, the biome-aware repairs produce wrong-for-biome
output. The mapping table should be reviewed before publishing
manuscript figures.
