PART 2: TRAINING STABILITY INCIDENT AND CORRECTIONS
================================================================================

First training attempts (2026-05-29) on the locally implemented
CfmTrainingLoop with hyperparameters LearningRate=1e-4, BatchSize=32,
NumSteps=2000 exhibited two failure modes across separate runs:

    Run A: loss descended from 1.07 at step 0 to 0.69 at step 200 (healthy)
           before being interrupted.

    Run B: loss was NaN at step 0 and remained NaN through the entire run.

Root cause analysis: training was unstable because there was no gradient
clipping and no random seed. The varying initial model weights between
runs produced different gradient magnitudes; some initializations resulted
in immediate gradient explosion and NaN values, others were temporarily
stable but oscillated near the loss floor.

Three corrections were applied to CfmTrainingLoop:

    1. torch.manual_seed(42) at the start of Run, before model construction.
       Makes the entire run deterministic for debugging and reproducibility.

    2. torch.nn.utils.clip_grad_norm_(model.parameters(), max_norm: 0.5)
       between loss.backward() and optimizer.step(). Caps the global
       gradient norm so no optimizer step can drive weights to infinity.

    3. LearningRate lowered from 1e-4 to 5e-5 in PcgFlowMatchingTrainEntryPoint.
       Smaller per-step updates reduce the chance of overshoot on top of the
       gradient clipping.

These three changes together produced the first stable training run
described in Part 3.

PART 5: FAILURE-MODE DISTRIBUTION ANALYSIS
================================================================================

Aggregated counts across all 10 generated chunks (3920 tile positions):

    Failure mode                Count  Percentage   Comment
    -------------------------   -----  -----------  ------------------------
    DiscontinuousGround         46     59.7         dominant
    FloatingEnemy               12     15.6
    BrokenPipeHorizontal        6      7.8
    BrokenPipeTopLeft           6      7.8
    BrokenPipeTopRight          4      5.2
    BrokenBulletBill            3      3.9          rare
    Total                       77     100.0

Mean violations per chunk:     7.7
Mean violation rate per tile:  1.96 percent

Interpretation:

    1. DiscontinuousGround is the dominant failure mode (60 percent of
       violations). The model knows there should be a ground row but does
       not consistently fill every column. Future iterations should target
       this specifically.

    2. FloatingEnemy is the second most frequent. The model places enemies
       where they look plausible (next to ground or near platforms) but
       sometimes drops them in the air without a supporting tile below.

    3. Pipe-related failures (top-left, top-right, horizontal) appear at
       similar rates, suggesting the model has not learned the multi-tile
       pipe structure as a unified concept.

    4. BulletBill failures are rare. Either the model handles them well or
       BulletBill tiles are rare in the training data (the latter is more
       likely; bullet bills only appear in late Mario levels).

These observations satisfy the qualitative evaluation direction (Opcao 2)
chosen with Prof. Elder Rizzon Santos on 2026-05-04: classify failure
types rather than count a single violation total. The data above gives
direct material for Chapter 4 of the manuscript.
